# Core Talk

**Draw a random topic. Research it. Speak about it for one minute. Listen back.**

Core Talk is a free speaking-practice app for students and aspirants. It gives you uncommon topics from 17 fields, so you practise explaining ideas clearly instead of repeating the same ones.

**Try it now: https://core-talk.github.io/core-talk/**

![Core Talk screenshot](screenshot.png)

## How it works

1. Choose the fields you want to practise.
2. Draw a topic. You also get a speaking twist, like "explain it to a ten-year-old".
3. Research the topic. The timer is yours to set (default 10 minutes).
4. Speak for one minute while the app records you.
5. Get a score out of 100 with a short written review, then play your recording back and spot what to fix.

## How scoring works

Out of 100: pace and length (20), fluency (20), pauses (15), depth and examples (20), topic coverage (15), and structure (10). Pace and pauses come from your voice. The rest comes from the transcript. The score is an estimate and does not check whether your facts are correct. Record in a quiet place for the most precise score.

Topics never repeat, and the sidebar lists everything you have already spoken on. Speaking on consecutive days builds a streak.

## Fields covered

UPSC, Environment & Ecology, Mining & Geology, Technical Terms, Inventions, Science, Space & Defence, Computing & AI, Energy, Engineering & Machines, Health & Medicine, Business & Finance, Law & Ethics, Sports, Arts & Culture, Psychology & Society, Everyday Curiosities.

## Your privacy

Core Talk has no server and never receives your recordings. They are saved only in your own browser, and you can delete any recording, or clear them all, from inside the app.

To score your talk, the app uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. In Chrome and Edge this converts speech to text through the browser maker's online service (Google or Microsoft), under their privacy terms. Firefox and Safari don't support it, so they get a partial score.

## Free to use

Core Talk is free. There is nothing to sign up for and no payment.

## Add a topic

Know a good topic that is missing? You can suggest one:

1. Open the **Issues** tab and choose **New issue**.
2. Write the topic and the field it belongs to.

Topics that are specific, interesting, and explainable in a minute are the best ones.

## Run it yourself

Core Talk is a single file. Download `index.html` and open it in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari). Microphone access is needed for recording.
